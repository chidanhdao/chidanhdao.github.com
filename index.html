Welcome. I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Queens' University.

My current research examines household financial decisions, consumer credit, and the distributional consequences of financial products.

Current research

Job market paper: Rewards, Credit Access, and Inequality in the Credit Card Market

My research examines consumer credit markets, with a particular focus on how financial products shape household borrowing, payment choices, and welfare. I study the interaction between credit card rewards, liquidity provision, and revolving debt, and how these features affect consumers across the income distribution.
